Our Mission

Generative AI has become a rising threat in the workplace; with the capability to displace workers in favor of generating cheap, sub-par content for consumers. For those who have lost their jobs, or anyone seeking a work environment where their human skills are valued more than the quick and dirty output of "content", HandCraft is here to help you find humans seeking humans.

Not looking for a job? You can still read up on the effects Generative AI has had on the job market and the world at large, so you can be better educated in order to advocate against it whenever necessary.
