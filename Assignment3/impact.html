Race Record

The following is a list of all 14 races that Winning Ticket ran during his career, including their grades, distances, and Tickezo's placement. 11 out of the 14 races were graded races, with 6 of these graded races being G1s, the highest level of race in Japanese horse racing (keiba). Out of 6 wins, "only" one of them was in a G1, that being the highly prestigious Tokyo Yushun (Japanese Derby)
