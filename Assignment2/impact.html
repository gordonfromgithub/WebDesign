Negative Impacts of Generative AI

The negative effects of Generative AI extend far beyond the deteriation of its users' ability to think independently. Beyond scraping the work of artists without permission or compensation, in an effort to reduce the wages that large corporations must pay their artists, Generative AI has both a detrimental effect on the environment (with how many resources are required to keep data centers running) and a negative social aspect that borders on, if not outright crosses into, sexual assault.
